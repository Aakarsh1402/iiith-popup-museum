Interview with Prabhakar M

Prabhakar M joined the institute in 2007 and assumed his current role in
the Student Life Office (SLO) in 2019. Prior to this, he was associated
with the Admissions and Recruitment Office, and before his SLO role, he
served as the head of hostel administration.

Early Days and Research Labs (2007 onwards)

When he joined the college in 2007, only Nilgiri was the academic block,
and only two floors were being used. He initially worked in the CSTAR
lab, which was located in the place where the current CQST is. The CSTAR
lab was a very impactful lab; it started distributed computing
conferences like ICDCN (2009) and FSTTCS (2012). For their work
collaborations, labs like CSTAR used to get government grants and IITM
guidance, whereas labs like RRC used to have private tenders like
Nissan.

During that time, the place where we currently have mental health
counseling used to be the Centre of IT in Education. Back then, the
Centre of IT in Education used to work on the Virtual Labs
project—Prabhakar B. Sir (Science 1 prof), who came from Bharath
Engineering College, worked extensively on that. Projects like Kidosoft
and Satyam research also used to happen there. Around the campus area,
DLF was made in 2011–12 in Gachibowli.

Admissions and Recruitment Office

During his tenure in the admissions office in the 2010s era, people
undervalued the Dual Degree over BTech. His efforts were focused on
making people aware of the unique way IIIT does the Dual Degree through
counseling. The college also experimented with various ways in which
admissions could be done, such as WREE (Winter Research Entrance Exam,
where people were taken in during winter semesters as well for research
degrees) and the NOK admission scheme (NTSE, Olympiad, KVPY). Later, in
2018, the college's flagship admission exam, UGEE, was started, for
which thorough counseling was done in H119 and H105.

Hostel Administration (2016)

Just after his time in the admissions and recruitment office, he took
over as the head of hostel administration in 2016. Here, he started the
massive work of digitization of all processes, report generation,
register sheets, and utility infrastructure, including hostel-related
electricity and water bills. His efforts led to the making of IMS in
2017, which became the go-to platform for room allocation, room
shifting, and other hostel-related work.

The institute has always been innovative and agile in adopting new
tech—it was one of the first customers of Atomberg (an IITB startup) in
2018, and all the fans we see across the hostels and campus today are
from 2018 due to the college's quick adoption. Around the hostels, there
also used to be an Honesty Shop where people used to donate and buy used
things, or grab some refreshments.

Student Life Office (2019 onwards) and Campus Culture

Prabhakar M assumed his current role in the Student Life Office in 2019,
though he was exposed to student trips in his very first year itself
when a student invited him on a batch trip. Since then, he has
accompanied students on almost all batch trips. They used to take
3rd-year students on 5-day trips to places like Vizag, Gokarna, and
Ooty. One interesting fact from these trips is that Purnima Resorts in
Gokarna actually built a separate building to accommodate the students
when the 2009 batch trip was scheduled there. However, post-COVID, these
trips stopped due to the nightmarish amount of logistical problems that
arose, alongside the batch strength increasing to nearly 300, which
became prohibitive for the institute.

Campus life and infrastructure have evolved significantly since those
earlier years:

* Sports and Grounds: Earlier, the ground in the place of Himalaya used
to be a cricket ground where faculty and students played together,
fostering a nice, organic relationship. There also used to be a gate
near the Felicity ground for students to access sports facilities at the
GMC Balayogi Stadium, where people used to line up for swimming pool
access.

* Events and Clubs: Convocations used to happen on the football ground
where they would put up tarpaulin tents. Earlier, we also had dedicated
halls for clubs like the dance club, music club, and a parliament room
in the Srujana Cultural Centre, alongside a playschool in Srujana.
Later, Naren Akash from the first batch of UGEE made great efforts to
form the Club Council, revamping the whole structure of clubs and
creating many new ones.
