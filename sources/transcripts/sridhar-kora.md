# Interview: Sridhar Kora (Hostel office in-charge since 2018)

> Source file: `raw/interviews_corpus/sridhar kora.docx` (converted to text for reading; the original is the authority).

Sridhar Kora succeeded the longstanding hostel office in-charge, Sri B.
Lakshmi Narayana. Having previously worked at institutions such as ICFAI
Business School and BITS Pilani Hyderabad (for 8 months) as a hostel
in-charge, he holds a diploma in mechanical engineering and an MBA. Upon
joining in 2018, he underwent a 10-day orientation program. [One sentence removed by the team for privacy.]
Drawing from his
mechanical engineering background, he has actively spearheaded technical
and operational innovations across the palash hostel.

Earlier, when the institute catered to only a few hundred students,
solar water heaters alone met the campus hot water demand. As student
strength grew, however, this capacity proved insufficient. Electric
geysers were deployed for a period, but their inconvenient heating time
caused bottlenecks during the morning rush. Consequently, the team
explored alternatives, visiting BITS Pilani Hyderabad to examine
innovative heat pump systems, which were subsequently installed on
campus and permanently resolved the issue.

LAN connectivity was also expanded across the campus as bandwidth and
access requirements grew. Older furniture, such as S-type wire-mesh
chairs, was replaced with ergonomic cushioned seating. Recently, the
D-block washrooms were completely renovated ahead of the UG2k25 batch.
Room occupancy has transitioned over time from single occupancy to
double sharing, and now to triple sharing, which frequently leads to
day-to-day roommate friction. During vacation periods, visiting project
interns are accommodated in six-sharing rooms equipped with bunk beds -
a rather intensive setup.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, both classes and examinations transitioned
to the CodeTantra platform, making IIITH the first institution in
Hyderabad to shift entirely to an online operational mode.
