# Interview: SLN Shastry (early staff, engineering/estate)

> Source file: `raw/interviews_corpus/sln shastry.docx` (converted to text for reading; the original is the authority).

In an interview, SLN Shastry shared that the government initially lent
them an unfinished building, Nilgiri, for classrooms, labs, and all
academic work, with faculty rooms located on the first floor. For
student housing, the Vindhya building served as temporary accommodation
for boys, while the Judge's Quarters were allotted for girls. When road
widening in front of the campus threatened to remove four banyan trees,
SLN Shastry took the initiative to get them transplanted instead:
Navjeevan (which was transplanted three times—from the main road to
Bakul Nivas, and finally to OBH), Jagruthi, a third tree in the Judge's
Quarters area, and a fourth near Buddha Nivas. He also recalled the
concept of a "User Professor," where a faculty member was appointed to
oversee the hostels and suggest practical details like furniture and
flooring. Additionally, he noted key campus spaces, including Bodh
Bhawan (Himalaya) for lecture halls, Vritti Bhavan for research, and
Nathula Pass, which served as the research street.
